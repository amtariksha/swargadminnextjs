Section 1

Accounting

A full GST accounting system inside the admin — invoicing, double-entry ledgers, purchase-bill capture with photo scanning (OCR), GST returns, bank reconciliation, and a two-way bridge to Tally. It keeps its own books but reads live orders, purchases and stock from the business. Find it under the Accounting group in the sidebar.

Two things must be right or nothing invoices — silently. On the Overview page, Accounting must read Enabled, and the Supplier state code must be set (Karnataka = 29). A missing state code is the number-one cause of "invoices aren't generating," and it shows no error.

Overview & setup New

Accounting → Settings → Overview /accounting

The control room: turn accounting on/off, set the company's GST identity, and jump to every other screen from the quick-link cards.

  1. Open Accounting → Settings → Overview.
  2. Check the top-right toggle. If it reads Accounting Disabled, click it once so it turns green and reads Accounting Enabled. Nothing invoices or posts while it's off.
  3. Fill Supplier identity (org GST profile): Legal name, Trade name, GSTIN, and — critically — Supplier state code (Karnataka = 29; the field is marked required). This code decides whether tax splits into CGST+SGST or IGST.
  4. Optionally set Default HSN and Default rate % (used only until products are mapped on the HSN page).
  5. Click Save profile.
  6. Use the quick-link cards to jump to Customers, HSN & Rates, Invoices, Ledgers, Tally, Bank Recon, B2C Consolidation or Reminders.
Before you start: you need the accounting role permission. The Supplier state code is mandatory.
Good to know
  • Accounting Enabled is fail-closed — off means no invoices anywhere, and no error is shown.
  • GSTIN auto-uppercases; the state code is 2 digits.

Chart of Accounts

Accounting → Chart of Accounts /accounting/accounts

The tree of account groups and ledgers with live balances, and where you create new ledgers, groups, banks, tax ledgers, suppliers and customers.

  1. Open Accounting → Chart of Accounts.
  2. Use the left Groups rail to drill into a group and its sub-groups; click a group to see only its ledgers, or Show all for the full chart.
  3. Read the top Total Debit / Credit bar and each group's subtotal to sanity-check.
  4. To add a ledger, click New Ledger, pick the Group, enter an Opening balance, choose Debit or Credit, then Create.
  5. Use New Group, New Bank (pre-targets Bank Accounts) or New Tax Ledger (pre-targets Duties & Taxes) as needed.
  6. New Supplier creates a vendor and auto-adds its payable ledger; New Customer jumps to the Customers page for a GST profile.
Tip: creating a supplier here makes its Sundry Creditors ledger automatically — you don't add it by hand.

Vouchers & journal entries

Accounting → Transactions → Vouchers /accounting/vouchers

Lists every journal entry — auto-posted and manual — and lets you hand-post a balanced double-entry voucher.

  1. Open Accounting → Transactions → Vouchers to see all entries (Date, Number, Type, Party, Narration, Source, Amount).
  2. Click + New Voucher.
  3. Pick a kind: Payment, Contra, Expense, Income or Journal.
  4. For Payment/Contra: choose "Paid To" and "Paid From" accounts and the Amount.
  5. For Expense/Income: choose the bank/cash account, then add ledger lines with + add line.
  6. For Journal: choose the document type (Journal / Credit Note / Debit Note) and fill the Dr/Cr grid until the footer reads balanced.
  7. Set the Date and optional Narration, then click Post voucher.
Don't record customer receipts here. The Receipt tab sends you to Customer Ledgers instead, so the money is allocated against the right open invoices. The server rejects an unbalanced journal.

Invoices — view, cancel, credit/debit note, reprice New

Accounting → Transactions → Invoices /accounting/invoices

The GST system of record. Invoices are created automatically when B2B deliveries are marked delivered, or by the consolidation jobs — this screen isn't where you type a new one; it's where you find, view and correct them.

  1. Open Invoices; set Status (All / Issued / Cancelled), a date range, or search a number/customer, then Apply.
  2. Click a row to open the full invoice.
  3. Click PDF for the printable GST invoice; click Ledger to jump to that customer's statement.
  4. To return/adjust: click Issue Credit Note (reduces what they owe) or Issue Debit Note (increases it), enter the adjusted quantity per line, a Reason and a Note date, then confirm.
  5. To fix a wrong price: click Reprice, edit the unit prices, then Save & reissue — this saves the new agreed price for that customer and cancels-and-reissues a fresh GST-numbered invoice.
  6. To void: click Cancel, add a reason, and confirm.
Irreversible Cancel keeps the invoice number (never reused) and posts a reversing ledger entry — it can't be undone. Credit/Debit note and Reprice are only on issued B2B tax invoices, not on B2C-consolidated or already-cancelled ones; a note's adjusted quantity can never exceed the invoiced quantity.

Purchases / Bills — review, import, scan & correct New

Accounting → Transactions → Purchases / Bills /accounting/purchases

Review raw-material purchase bills — typed, imported, or photo-scanned — then post them as stock plus a Purchase voucher; and correct a bill after it's posted.

  1. Open Purchases / Bills. Use the tabs To review, Posted, Rejected, All.
  2. Click the pencil to open a draft: check Vendor, Material, Account head (GL ledger; blank = Purchase account), Qty, Unit price, GST rate %, Supply (Intra/Inter-state), HSN, Invoice no.
  3. If a cyan fat-pricing hint appears, click Apply suggested price to use fat × rate.
  4. Click Approve & Post (posts stock + Purchase voucher + Tally queue) or Reject. Use Save changes to keep editing.
  5. To handle many at once, tick rows on To review and use Approve selected (n) / Reject selected (n).
  6. To bulk-load: Import bills → Spreadsheet (CSV/Excel) (upload, review the dry-run, "Import n drafts") or Scan bills (photos) (up to 20 images/PDFs; OCR creates drafts).
  7. To fix a posted bill: open it, click Correct cost, enter a Reason (required) + optional date, then Post correction.
Correcting a posted bill: it reverses the live ledger entry and re-posts the new amount in the correction period; the original is preserved. If it reports "ledger sync is pending", do not re-submit — it self-heals; re-submitting double-applies.
OCR badges: red (under 50%) or amber (below the auto-post threshold) both mean "open the photo and verify before approving." Bills at/above the confidence threshold (Tally settings, default 0.9) post automatically; lower ones wait in To review.

Customers & HSN rates New

Accounting → Transactions → Customers /accounting/customers · HSN & Rates /accounting/hsn

Maintain each customer's GST identity (B2B vs B2C) and price overrides; and define HSN codes + GST rates so invoices calculate the right tax.

  1. On Customers, B2B loads immediately; expand B2C Customers for that (large) set. Search by name / phone / GSTIN.
  2. Click the pencil to edit: set Customer type, Credit terms (days), Legal name, GSTIN (sets B2B), PAN, Place of supply (state code), and addresses; click Save.
  3. For a B2B customer, click the amber Tag icon or Manage B2B price overrides to set agreed per-product prices; click the book icon to open their ledger.
  4. On HSN & Rates, click Add HSN code (e.g. 0401), then on the HSN card click Rate to add a GST %; tick Set as default rate for this HSN for the usual one.
  5. Switch to Map Products, choose an HSN+rate, tick Unmapped only to find products with no rate, select them, and click Map n product(s).
Notes: entering a GSTIN auto-flags the customer as B2B. B2B prices are GST-inclusive (tax is back-calculated). Until a product is HSN-mapped, invoices fall back to the org default rate.

B2C consolidation New Default-off

Overview card /accounting · review & post /accounting/b2c-consolidation

Rolls up many tiny unregistered (B2C) retail sales into one consolidated GST invoice instead of one per sale.

  1. On the Overview page, find the B2C consolidation card.
  2. To enable the monthly roll-up, click Monthly consolidation so it reads Enabled, set the Run time (IST), and Save. The job runs on the 1st for the prior month.
  3. On the 1st, open Accounting → Transactions → B2C Consolidation, pick the Period month, and review the Taxable / CGST / SGST / IGST / Total tiles and per-slab lines.
  4. For a Draft, click Post consolidated invoice and confirm.
Two separate switches, both off by default. Monthly consolidation is self-serve here. The daily switch — which changes how every B2C sale is invoiced — is read-only on the card on purpose: it's a back-end change, so raise it with Pradeep, not on-screen. Operator step Posting a consolidated run is irreversible and needs the B2C Consolidation User Id setting configured.

Reports & GST returns New: Sales Register

Accounting → Reports /accounting/reports/… · GST Returns /accounting/gst-returns

Read the books and generate portal-ready GST returns.

  1. Trial Balance — pick the financial year; confirm the badge reads Balanced. "Out of balance" means investigate a posting before filing anything.
  2. Balance Sheet — Assets vs Liabilities & Equity as on today (the P&L net profit is carried into the equity side); confirm Balanced.
  3. Profit & Loss — pick the FY; read Income vs Expenses down to the Net Profit/Loss line.
  4. Day Book — pick a date to see every voucher posted that day, expanded to its Dr/Cr lines (great for auditing what hit the ledgers).
  5. Sales Register New — pick a month range; read the full GST break-up per invoice and the "Total (issued only)" footer (cancelled invoices are listed but excluded).
  6. GST Returns — enter the period as YYYY-MM, click Generate GSTR-1 / GSTR-3B / GSTR-9 (annual), then Download the JSON. To reconcile input tax, upload the portal's GSTR-2B JSON and click Import & reconcile for matched / mismatch / missing counts.
Before you start: invoices and purchases for the period must already be posted, or the returns come out empty.

Customer ledgers & recording a receipt

Accounting → Reports → Ledgers /accounting/ledgers

A customer's running statement with outstanding balance and ageing — and the correct place to record a payment received, so it clears the right invoices.

  1. Open Ledgers, choose B2B or B2C, then search and select the customer.
  2. Read the Outstanding tile and the Current / 31–60 / 61–90 / 90+ ageing tiles, then the entry-by-entry statement.
  3. Click Record receipt: enter Amount, Mode (Online / Cash / Wallet / Bank Transfer) and a Reference (UTR / cheque / txn id).
  4. Under Allocate to open invoices, enter how much of the receipt clears each open invoice (leave blank for an on-account receipt), then Record receipt.
Tip: record receipts here, not in the Voucher modal, so the receivable is matched to the correct invoices. Allocated amounts can't exceed the receipt total. You can deep-link here from an invoice's Ledger button.

Bank reconciliation New

Accounting → Settings → Bank Recon /accounting/bank-reconciliation

Import a bank statement, let the system suggest matches to invoices/ledgers, and confirm each row so a voucher posts against the mapped bank ledger — nothing posts unreviewed.

  1. Open Bank Recon. Optionally pick a Bank account (or click Manage to add one and map it to its GL ledger).
  2. Click Upload statement and choose a CSV / Excel / PDF file.
  3. Click the imported statement row to open the review panel (tabs: Suggested, Needs review, Reconciled / ignored).
  4. On Suggested, click Confirm per row (or Confirm all suggested).
  5. For unmatched rows, click Match (pick an invoice or ledger), Suspense (park against Bank Suspense) or Ignore. Use Assign all → Suspense to clear a batch.
Before you start: add at least one bank account and map it to a GL bank ledger (via Manage), so reconciled rows have somewhere to post.
Auto-match: Razorpay settlement deposits are matched automatically once the bank ledger is configured. Money-in rows can match an invoice or ledger; money-out rows go to a ledger only.

Tally bridge, reconcile & opening balances New: import Default-off

Tally /accounting/tally-settings · Reconcile /accounting/reconcile · Opening Balances /accounting/opening-balances

Configure the Tally bridge, turn posting/import on or off, watch the sync queue, pull data back from Tally, and enter opening balances.

  1. On Tally → Settings, toggle Posting to Tally on only after the Windows bridge host is verified; toggle Importing from Tally on to allow the Import tab.
  2. Set Purchase auto-post confidence (0–1, default 0.9) and fill the Bridge connection + Company & ledger mappings (Sales, Default debtor, CGST, SGST, IGST, Round-off, B2C ledgers). Click Save settings.
  3. On Sync queue, filter by status and click Retry on any failed/pending voucher.
  4. On Import from Tally, run a day-book import (From/To + Mode: Sync skips Swarg-owned vouchers, Seed imports everything for a one-time cutover), or take a Trial-balance snapshot.
  5. On Opening Balances, pick the FY, add ledgers, type each Debit/Credit until the footer reads balanced, then Save draft or Finalise & post.
When posting is off, vouchers pile up as "pending" — that's expected, not a bug. Use Seed mode only for a one-time cutover; use Sync for ongoing pulls so Swarg's own vouchers aren't re-imported. Finalising an opening voucher when one already exists voids the prior one and re-posts (a yellow banner warns you).

Payment reminders

Accounting → Settings → Reminders /accounting/reminders

Cadence rules (relative to an invoice's due date) that automatically nudge customers about overdue payments through the notification engine.

  1. Open Reminders and click Add rule.
  2. Enter Offset days (negative = before due, 0 = on due date, positive = overdue) — the label under the field confirms the meaning.
  3. Toggle the Channels (whatsapp / email / sms / bell), set a Template title, tick Active, and save.
  4. Check the Recent sends log to confirm reminders are going out.
Before you start: a matching notification template (e.g. an approved WhatsApp template) should exist for the channel and title you use.

Section 2

Last-Mile dispatch

Everything between "an order exists" and "a box reaches the door": truck-route drop points, last-mile driver assignments, the daily Delivery List, packing, and returns. The daily rhythm is — production packs against the pre-packing quantity check, the Delivery List is generated, the truck drops boxes at each drop point where last-mile drivers collect them, drivers mark deliveries in the app, and admins monitor and handle exceptions.

Drop points & warehouses New

Settings → Drop Points /drop-points · Settings → Warehouses /settings/warehouses

The physical truck-route hand-off points where the truck drops boxes for last-mile drivers to collect — with GPS pin, route order, photos, and which warehouse supplies each stop.

  1. First, under Settings → Warehouses, create a warehouse for each supply source and choose its supply mode — Truck (with the truck driver's name) or Factory/Dairy pickup.
  2. Open Settings → Drop Points (or type "drop" in the Search menu box) and click Add New.
  3. Enter a Title (required, e.g. the apartment/landmark), optional Latitude/Longitude for driver navigation, an optional Route Order number, Notes, and pick the Warehouse (supply).
  4. Click Create — the dialog stays open so you can Add Photo (up to 5 reference images).
  5. Later, click Edit on a row to change it. The table shows each stop's Route Order, assigned Drivers count and photo count.
Only create "stock" hand-off points here. B2B shop stops are a different kind (created through the B2B customer setup) — never delete a shop-type row from this page. A drop point with no warehouse shows no supply mode; factory_pickup renders as the "Dairy pickup" chip, truck warehouses as "Truck · <driver>". Deleting a stop that still has drivers/orders can break assignments — reassign first.

Assign a last-mile driver & grant app capabilities New

People → Drivers /drivers

Attach a last-mile (milk) driver to the drop point they collect from, and grant delivery-app capabilities such as Milk Delivery, Truck Deliveries, Collection Pickup, Scan Bill or Dairy Receipt.

  1. Open People → Drivers and click Edit on the driver's row.
  2. Set Driver Type to Last-mile (the drop-point picker only appears for last-mile drivers; Truck and Day are the other types).
  3. Pick the driver's Drop Point from the dropdown that appears (or leave "— Not assigned —").
  4. Optionally toggle Driver Location On to require GPS capture when they mark a delivery.
  5. Under Delivery App Capabilities, toggle extras: Milk Delivery, Truck Deliveries, Collection Pickup, Scan Bill (purchase capture), Dairy Receipt (Milk Received), Collection Approval.
  6. Click Update, then tell the driver to log out and back in so changes take effect.
The system won't let you assign a last-mile driver to a B2B shop drop point ("Drop point must be a stock hand-off point") — shops are truck-delivered. Capability and location changes always require a log-out/log-in.

Delivery List — the daily dispatch hub New

Deliveries → Delivery List /delivery-list

Generate the day's deliveries from active subscriptions/orders, then read and manage the list across six tabs: Customer Orders, Routewise Products, Packing List, Dairy Pickup, Drop Points, Shops.

  1. Open Deliveries → Delivery List and pick the date (defaults to today).
  2. On Customer Orders, click Generate List, enter the passcode when prompted (type TODAY for today, or the 8-digit YYYYMMDD for a future date), and confirm. A progress overlay shows created vs skipped.
  3. Use Check deliveries to dry-run tomorrow's generation — it lists low-balance/partial orders and orders with no driver, creating nothing.
  4. Switch tabs to read the list different ways (products per driver, total qty per product, pickup groups, drop points, shops).
  5. On Customer Orders, filter with the checkboxes (Delivered Only, Not Delivered Only, Different Qty Delivered) or the All-Drivers dropdown; tick rows + Mark Selected Delivered for bulk.
  6. Click the blue pencil (Qty Edit) to change a scheduled quantity (0 = skip); click the amber warning icon to set a non-delivery reason.
  7. Export CSV exports any tab. Delete List is passcode-gated (deleting today's list needs the longer safety code).
Reading the columns: Qty = scheduled; Del Qty = what the driver actually marked (red when different, amber strike-through when 0). Driver marks never overwrite the scheduled qty — only Qty Edit does. Generate is safe to re-run: it skips existing entries. B2B shops live on the Shops tab but are still counted in Packing List totals.
Before you start: the auto-generate cron normally builds tomorrow's list overnight — manual Generate is for corrections or a missed run. Subscriptions and driver assignments must exist first.

Drop-point & Shop tabs — monitor the truck stage New

Deliveries → Delivery List → Drop Points / Shops tabs /delivery-list

See the truck's box hand-off per drop point and per B2B shop, and push undelivered items to the day-time pool.

  1. On Drop Points, each stop is one collapsed line (route order, title, a Dairy-pickup / "Truck · <driver>" chip, customer count, total qty).
  2. Click a line to expand it — per-customer delivery lines, each driver's "boxes" with a Delivered / Short / Not-delivered outcome, and any returnable-box photos and counts.
  3. On Shops, each B2B shop card shows route order, customer, truck driver, per-product delivered quantities, and a Pending / Done / "In Day Pool" status.
  4. If a shop has undelivered lines, click Send to day pool → confirm Send to Day Net. A day driver can then claim and deliver it (billed by GST invoice, no wallet).
  5. For a single missed morning order, use Send to Day Net in that row's Day Net column on Customer Orders.
These tabs are read-only monitors for the truck stage — the actual marks and returnable photos are entered by drivers in the app. "Send to day pool" is the only write action; clicking it twice is treated as "already in pool," not an error.

Pre-packing forecast & packing runs New

Archive → Pre-Packing List /production-delivery · Accounting → Inventory → Production → Packing /production/packing

Decide how much to pack tomorrow morning (a dry run), and log how many packets were sealed from bulk as a reconciliation record.

  1. On Pre-Packing List, click Run quantity check — a dry run of tomorrow's generation (nothing saved) showing exact Qty to pack per product, net of wallet allocation, next to a 28-day forecast and a Δ column.
  2. Use the Delivery Truck tab for the truck-load summary + per-driver route groups; Packing List and Dairy Pickup for a generated date's totals.
  3. To record a packing run: Production → Packing → Record packing, choose the Packed product, set Packed from (Intermediate made in-house / Raw material repacked), pick the Source, enter Packets packed + Date, then Record packing.
Both are non-destructive. Pre-Packing never writes anything — it's just the tool for deciding quantities. Recording a packing run does not change sellable stock; it's purely a reconciliation/wastage log. The 28-day forecast covers subscriptions only; "~" marks products with under 14 days of history.

Returns & refunds · Day orders New

Orders → Refunds & Returns /returns-refunds · Orders → Day Orders /day-orders

Track returnable-packaging pickups (bottles, crates) and refund the deposit to the wallet; and manage one-off day-time (11 AM–6 PM) deliveries including cash settlement.

  1. On Refunds & Returns, set the Refund Mode toggle — auto credits the wallet on pickup, manual requires admin approval.
  2. Filter by status; for a Pending approval row click Approve (credits the wallet, shows the new balance).
  3. For a return still in flight you want to settle now, click Refund to issue the wallet credit manually.
  4. On Day Orders, review the Orders / Revenue / Not-delivered / Collected / To-collect tiles.
  5. Click New Order for a one-time day order, or B2B / Catering for an institutional order (the modal also saves the org as a reusable B2B customer).
  6. When a day order is paid in cash, use Mark cash — you can now attach a cash note (who collected) and a payment screenshot.
Irreversible The manual Refund button issues the wallet credit immediately — there's no undo. Undelivered morning orders and B2B shops arrive in Day Orders automatically when someone clicks "Send to Day Net". Set up returnable items first under Settings → Packaging Types.

Link a lead to an existing customer New

LMS → Leads → open a lead /lms/leads/[id]

When a "new lead" is really an existing customer messaging from a second number, record that number as an alternate on the real customer so future logins, orders and messages resolve to them.

  1. Open the lead from LMS → Leads.
  2. Find the Link to existing customer card (amber link icon) — it only shows while the lead isn't already Converted or Duplicate.
  3. In Customer ID, type the existing customer's backend user ID (look it up under People → Customers).
  4. Click Link and confirm the prompt.
Irreversible from the UI — double-check the Customer ID before clicking Link. It records the lead's phone as an alternate number on the customer (it doesn't merge or delete order history) and marks the lead a duplicate.

Section 3

CRM & leads

Three related but separate areas. CRM is the phone-call desk for existing customers. LMS is the newer lead pipeline plus AI (insights, at-risk, digests). Business Pulse is a CEO financial dashboard. Live WhatsApp messaging is a fourth, separate module. In short: CRM = calls, LMS = leads + AI, WhatsApp = chat.

Call worklist — "Customers due for a call"

CRM → Worklist /crm/worklist

The daily relationship-call to-do list: existing customers with no completed call inside the cadence window, plus any follow-ups due today.

  1. Open CRM → Worklist (opening CRM lands here).
  2. Read the sub-heading for how many are due and the cadence (e.g. "no completed call in the last 30 days").
  3. Scan each row: Reason badge (orange "Follow-up" / blue "Due for call"), Customer, Phone, Route, an activity strip (7/15/30/60/90/180-day chips), Last Call, and Wallet (red under ₹250).
  4. Click a row or the purple Start call button to open the guided call screen.
  5. Use Refresh after logging calls. If empty, read the "Why is this empty?" panel.
Worklist is about existing customers (relationship / reactivation care). New prospects live under LMS → Leads. Cadence is controlled by the crm_call_cadence_days setting.

Guided call screen — log feedback

Opened from Worklist "Start call", All Feedback, or a customer's Feedback tab /crm/call/<userId>

A one-screen call cockpit: the customer's context, the script to read, and a 9-section checklist to record what they say.

  1. At the top, review the context card — tap the phone number to dial, see wallet (red under ₹250), route, and the activity strip.
  2. Open the "📞 Before the call — read the script" panel; use the call-type toggle (Feedback / Reactivation / any active script type) to load the right script.
  3. Work down the numbered During the call sections: Greeting, Problems, Product feedback, Delivery feedback, Preferences (call/delivery time, ring-bell, drop place), App feedback, Care notes, Occupation.
  4. In section 9 Call outcome, choose a Status (required): Done, No Response, Call Later, Discontinued or Invalid Number. Set the Call date.
  5. If you pick Call Later, a required Follow-up date appears — that date puts the customer back on the Worklist.
  6. Click Save call.
Saving fails (with a toast) if you don't choose a Status, or choose Call Later without a follow-up date. At least one Call Script must exist for the chosen type, or the panel shows "No script configured".

Feedback log & call scripts New: custom types

CRM → All Feedback /crm/feedback · CRM → Call Scripts /crm/scripts

The searchable master log of every call, and the editor where a manager writes the scripts callers read.

  1. On All Feedback, filter by Status, Call type, and date range, or free-text search across customer, caller and notes.
  2. Click the chevron on a row to expand long-text fields inline; click elsewhere on a row to open it for editing.
  3. To log fresh, click New feedback (or New reactivation), search the customer, and select them.
  4. On Call Scripts, use the script-type buttons to select a script, edit Title and Body (markdown, with a live preview), and Save script.
  5. To add a type, click Add Script, pick a preset or Other (custom)…, name it, fill Title/Body, then Add script.
New this month: call types are no longer capped at Feedback/Reactivation — any active script you add (Welcome, Seasonal, Offer, or a custom name up to 20 lowercase chars) automatically becomes a call-type option and an All-Feedback filter, with no developer change. Each customer's history also shows on their profile's Feedback tab (People → Customers → open a customer).

Lead pipeline — sources, owner, score New

LMS → Leads /lms/leads · open one /lms/leads/<id>

The prospect pipeline: every lead from WhatsApp, website, stalls, app installs, referrals or manual entry, with its source, owner, score and stage.

  1. Pick the pipeline with the B2C / B2B / All tabs.
  2. Filter by Source (this is the lead-source attribution), status chips (New, Contacted, Qualified, Converted, Lost, Duplicate), or the search box.
  3. Toggle sort between Newest and Hottest (ranks by the rule-based 0–100 score).
  4. Read the columns — including Expires (days until an untouched open lead auto-lapses to Lost at 30 days).
  5. Click Sync from contacts to backfill leads from your WhatsApp contacts (safe to re-run), or New lead to add manually.

New this month: B2C/B2B tabs, an Owner column with assign-to-staff, rule-based Score with "Hottest" sort, tag editing, and "Sync from contacts." Assigning an owner can fire an opt-in WhatsApp "new lead" alert to that salesperson. Leads live in a separate store from the main customer/order database.

People, RFM & At-Risk Today New

LMS → People /lms/people · At-Risk Today /lms/at-risk · Today /lms · Insights /lms/insights · System & Jobs /lms/system

A unified contact list enriched with RFM segment, health score and churn risk — plus the AI action feed and a ranked churn-save call list.

  1. On People, filter by RFM Segment (Champions, Loyal, Promising, At-Risk, Hibernating, Lost) and Churn risk to build call lists.
  2. On At-Risk Today, work the numbered list top-to-bottom; each row shows risk, health, days-since-last-order and a "Suggested:" next action, with one-tap Call / WhatsApp.
  3. On Today, read the five metric cards and act on each AI insight with Approve / Snooze / Dismiss; click Run insights to generate a fresh batch now.
  4. If segment/health columns are blank, open System & Jobs and click Recompute now to rebuild scores from real order history.
Two different call lists — don't confuse them. At-Risk Today is driven by RFM/health (churn saves); the CRM Worklist is driven by call cadence + follow-ups (routine care). Danger On System & Jobs, never click Seed demo on production — it overwrites everyone's scores with random values; use Recompute now instead.

Turning on the nightly AI automations Default-off Operator step

Back-end app settings + Settings → Automation /settings/automation/runs (run history)

The Phase-4 AI/marketing automations all shipped off. There are four separate switches.

  1. Decide which to enable: RFM Nightly (score recompute), Insights Agent Nightly (AI insights), CEO Digest (weekly WhatsApp digest, Mondays), Daily Sales Nudge (each lead-owner's hottest leads).
  2. Have an engineer flip the chosen setting from 0 to 1 — these are not on the on-screen Automation toggle page; they're set in the database (seeded off by migration 103).
  3. Set the supporting values as needed (CEO Digest Time, ceo_digest_phone, Sales Nudge Time, LMS Nightly Run Time) and enable the parent LMS Nightly job.
  4. Ensure Agent Force + the LMS_ADMIN_* env link are configured, or the AI steps stay inert.
  5. Verify fires under Settings → Automation → View run history.
Nothing messages a customer, the CEO or sales staff until an operator flips the flag AND the WhatsApp templates + Agent Force are ready. You can always run RFM and Insights on demand from LMS → System & Jobs and LMS → Today without enabling any cron. The regular timed jobs that do appear on Settings → Automation (delivery list, low-balance, driver nudges) are toggled on-screen — the four AI switches are the exception.
